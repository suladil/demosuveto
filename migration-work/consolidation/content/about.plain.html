Our story

It started with one clinic on River Walk Avenue

Dr. Elena Marsh opened the first Paws & Partners clinic in Riverside in 2000 with two exam rooms, one technician and a simple promise: treat every pet like family and every owner like a partner in their care.

As our community grew, so did we. Today our four hospitals share one team, one record system and the same commitment to unhurried, compassionate medicine.

Our journey

Growing with our community

Each new hospital was opened where our clients asked us to be.

2000 · Riverside opens

Our first clinic opens downtown and later becomes our walk-in urgent care center.

2009 · Old Town joins

A beloved local practice joins the group and becomes our Cat Friendly hospital.

2016 · Lakeview opens

We bring care to the North Shore, with a dedicated advanced dental suite.

2026 · Northgate opens

Our newest hospital opens its doors and welcomes new clients from across the North Shore.

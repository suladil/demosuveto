New to Paws & Partners?

Your first visit in three easy steps

New clients receive a complimentary first wellness exam at any of our hospitals.*

  1. Choose a hospital & time

    Book online or call the location nearest you. Same-week appointments are usually available.

  2. Share your pet's history

    Complete the new-client form and upload previous records. We'll request them for you if you prefer.

  3. Meet your care team

    Enjoy a relaxed, thorough first exam and leave with a personalized wellness plan.

Claim Your Free Exam

*Complimentary exam fee for new clients. Vaccines, diagnostics and treatments are billed separately.
